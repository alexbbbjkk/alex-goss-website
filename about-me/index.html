About me

Hi, I'm Alex Goss

I'm 21 and I run my own digital marketing business, working hands-on with small and growing businesses.

Why I do this

Marketing that's thoughtful, honest and built around your business, not a package from a big agency.

I started this business because I believe small and growing businesses deserve marketing that's tailored to them, not a one-size-fits-all package with a rotating cast of account managers.

I've spent the years since leaving school working in fast-paced, hands-on environments before ever calling myself a marketer. I think that's exactly why I do this differently. I've had to earn trust quickly, learn on the job and take ownership of real outcomes, not just tick boxes. That's the standard I hold my client work to as well.

  • Direct, one-on-one collaboration, with no account managers or hand-offs
  • Clear reporting so you always know what's working
  • Flexible engagements, from one-off projects to ongoing support
My story so far

How I actually got here

Each step taught me something I still use in client work, from design and code to operations and compliance.

  1. School work placement

    Virgin Atlantic

    Building web pages with HTML and Python and designing digital images for their site. My first real taste of the crossover between design, code and marketing.

  2. Study

    T Level, Digital Production, Design and Development

    At Chichester College, alongside a 315-hour internship at Patterson Dental, where I digitally designed crowns and bridges for dental practices. A lesson in precision, client standards and technical work that has to be right first time.

  3. Alongside my studies

    General Assistant, Out of Bounds, then McDonald's

    A year and a half at McDonald's, where I was promoted from Crew Member to Shift Manager within three months. I ran shift plans, staff rotas and income and expenditure while still a teenager.

  4. Full-time role

    In-house Digital Marketer, National Parking Control

    Running SEO, PPC, email and social media, plus IT support and compliance work. I helped secure SafeContractor accreditation twice and contributed to the company reaching ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and Cyber Essentials Plus, and ranking #12 in the UK's Best Workplaces 2026.
