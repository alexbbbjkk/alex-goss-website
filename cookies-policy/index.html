What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're widely used to make websites work more effectively, and to provide information to the site owner.

How this site uses cookies

This site uses the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): these help me understand how visitors use the site, which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and how they found the site, so I can improve it. These cookies collect information in an anonymous or pseudonymous form.

This site does not currently use any advertising or retargeting cookies. If that changes in future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or be notified when a new cookie is set. You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically using Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Please note that blocking cookies may affect how parts of this site work.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time as the tools used on this site change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
