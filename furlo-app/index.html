What it does

Everything a boarding or dog walking business needs, in one app

📅

Bookings & Calendar

One calendar for every booking, holiday, and bank holiday, with separate views for boarding and dog walking. Book multiple pets from the same owner in one go, set up recurring bookings that repeat weekly, fortnightly, or monthly, and get automatic conflict detection so double-bookings never happen.

💳

Payments & Invoicing

Track exactly who owes what, deposits, part-payments, and full payments, with automatic invoice generation that includes your bank details and accepted payment methods, so chasing payments stops being a manual job.

🐾

Customer Experience

Customers can view their own bookings, see what they owe, and download invoices themselves. A shareable link lets new pet owners fill out their own pet's profile, and you control which parts they can update afterwards.

📋

Pet & Business Records

Full pet profiles, breed, vet details, insurance, and vaccination history, with vaccination due dates shown right on your calendar. Supports dogs, cats, and small animals, plus your own Terms & Conditions with digital signing from pet owners.

✉️

Communication

Automatic booking confirmation, update, and cancellation emails, plus a 24-hour booking reminder email that includes anything still owed, so fewer no-shows and fewer awkward payment conversations.
