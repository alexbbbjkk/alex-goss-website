What it does

Everything a pet boarding or dog walking business needs, in one app

📅

Bookings & calendar

One calendar for every booking, holiday and bank holiday, with separate views for boarding and dog walking. Book multiple pets from the same owner in one go, set up recurring bookings (weekly, fortnightly or monthly), and get automatic conflict detection so double-bookings never happen.

💳

Payments & invoicing

Track exactly who owes what, including deposits, part-payments and full payments. Invoices are generated automatically with your bank details and accepted payment methods, so chasing payments stops being a manual job.

🐾

Customer experience

Customers can view their own bookings, see what they owe and download invoices themselves. A shareable link lets new pet owners fill out their pet's profile, and you control which parts they can update afterwards.

📋

Pet & business records

Full pet profiles covering breed, vet details, insurance and vaccination history, with due dates shown on your calendar. Supports dogs, cats and small animals, plus your own Terms & Conditions with digital signing from pet owners.

✉️

Communication

Automatic booking confirmation, update and cancellation emails, plus a 24-hour reminder email that includes anything still owed. Fewer no-shows and fewer awkward payment conversations.
