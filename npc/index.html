Digital Marketer

National Parking Control Group Ltd · 2 years

I joined as the company's only in-house digital marketer, responsible for social media, SEO, paid ads, email and the CRM, with no team to hand things off to and no room for guesswork. Over two years I grew the company's social following from under 150 to over 3,000, ran SEO and Google Ads PPC campaigns that contributed to measurable increases in client acquisition, and built HTML-based e-shots to support outreach, all while managing the day-to-day CRM in HubSpot.

It isn't freelance client work yet, but it's real: live campaigns, a real budget, and results a business actually depended on, not a case study written after the fact.
