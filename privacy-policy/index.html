The short version

  • I only collect what you send me through the contact form, plus basic website analytics.
  • I use your details to reply to you and, if we work together, to deliver the service.
  • I don't sell your data. It's shared only with the providers that run this site and my inbox.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing me.

Who we are

This website is operated by Alex Goss, trading as Alex Goss Digital Marketing ("I", "me", "my"). I'm a sole trader based in the UK. For any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact me at alex_goss05@outlook.com.

What personal data I collect

I collect personal data in two ways:

  • Contact form: when you use the contact form on this site, I collect your name, email address and any message or details you choose to include.
  • Website analytics: this site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. This may include approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, pages visited and time spent on the site. See the Cookie Policy for more detail.

Why I collect it

I use contact form submissions to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss and provide marketing services to you. This is based on my legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about my services and, where a contract is formed, on performing that contract.

I use Google Analytics data to understand how the site is used and to improve it. Where the law requires consent for this, I rely on the consent you give through the site's cookie settings.

How long I keep it

Contact form submissions are kept for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry. If we go on to work together, they're kept for the length of that relationship plus a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and legal purposes, typically up to 6 years in line with UK tax record-keeping requirements. If we don't work together, I'll delete your enquiry details within 12 months unless you ask me to keep them or contact me again.

Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' default retention settings.

Who I share it with

I don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone. I share it only with the following providers, and only as needed to run this website and my business:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form submissions to my email inbox.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC), for website analytics.
  • Website hosting provider, which hosts this site.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data I hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • Ask me to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask me to delete your data
  • Object to, or restrict, how I use your data
  • Ask for your data in a portable format

To use any of these rights, email alex_goss05@outlook.com. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact
